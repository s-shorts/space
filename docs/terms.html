Shorts Scheduler: terms of service

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What this is

Shorts Scheduler is a private tool that its owner uses to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts at scheduled times. It is not offered to the public, has no user accounts and is provided free of charge with no advertising.

YouTube Terms of Service

Shorts Scheduler uses YouTube API Services. By using Shorts Scheduler to upload to YouTube, the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

Acceptable use

The tool may be used only to upload videos that the owner made or has the right to publish, and that follow YouTube's Community Guidelines and Instagram's rules. It must not be used to upload spam, misleading content or content that infringes anyone's rights.

Privacy

How information is handled is described in the privacy policy, which also refers to the Google Privacy Policy.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, without any warranty. Scheduled posts can be late or fail, and the owner is responsible for checking what was published.
